
# The <ins>Co</ins>mmon <ins>La</ins>ws of <ins>So</ins>cial <ins>B</ins>ehavior (CoLa-SoB)
> Kolasab [/ˈkoʊ.lɑ.sæb/ : KOH-lah-sab] Derrived from the acronym "CoLa-SoB"

  

**The Common Laws of Social Behavior** (aka **The Kolasab**) is a dictionary-like set of files that outlines The Common Laws of Social and human behavior. Its goal is to provide a standardized (yet witty) way to refer to natural phenomena in human interactions.

Whether you're trying to explain why people always talk over each other during debates, or why Kanye never got that fish-sticks joke (JK we still have no idea 😭), or why your math teacher wont shut up about his wife, **The Kolasab** has you covered. It’s a playful take on the (now spoken) rules that govern social behavior, wrapped up in a unique, easy-to-reference format.

## 📋 Overview
### **What is The Kolasab?**
- A collection of "laws" describing common patterns, behaviors, and interactions among us meatbags.

- Humorous (the witty kind) and easy to understand.
### **Purpose:**
- Provide a standardized way to refer to common human behaviors in social or professional settings.
- Perfect for casual conversations, psychology discussions, or just for laughs.
### **Content:**
- Dictionary-like entries format where each "law" is like a definition. Sorta like Merriam Webster
- Articals explaining how these laws interact with one another posted weekly.

## 📌 Getting Started
To use **The Kolasab**, simply browse or search the laws listed in the pages by interaction or name and reference them as needed. Each law describes a common human behavior or interaction that can be explained with humor and insight, and each artical describes how 2-5 laws can interact with each other.

### Example Law:

**Inverse Law of Authoritative Figures**:

When an authority figure tells you what to do, you’re inclined to do the opposite, almost immediately.

**Example Interaction:**

- Professor/Teacher: "Please don't say `moist`!"

- The class (in unison): "***Moist.***."
