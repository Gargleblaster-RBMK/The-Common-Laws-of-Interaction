# The Common Laws of Interaction (CLoI [Kloh-ie, /kloʊɪ/])

**The Common Laws of Interaction** (aka **CLoI**) is a humorous, dictionary-like set of files that outlines the common laws of interaction and human behavior. Its goal is to provide a standardized (yet funny) way to refer to natural phenomena in human interactions.

Whether you're trying to explain why people always talk over each other during debates, or why Kanye never got that fish sticks joke, **CLoI** has you covered. It’s a playful take on the (now spoken) rules that govern social behavior, wrapped up in a unique, easy-to-reference format.

---

## 📋 Overview

- **What is CLoI?**
  - A collection of "laws" describing common patterns, behaviors, and interactions among humans.
  - Humorous (the witty kind) and easy to understand.

- **Purpose:**
  - Provide a standardized way to refer to common human behaviors in social settings.
  - Perfect for casual conversations, psychology discussions, or just for laughs.

- **Content:**
  - A dictionary-like format where each "law" is explained like a definition.

---

## 📌 Getting Started

To use **CLoI**, simply browse the laws listed in the pages and reference them as needed. Each law describes a common human behavior or interaction that can be explained with humor and insight.

### Example Law:

**Inverse Law of Authority Figures**:  
When an authority figure tells you what to do, you’re inclined to do the opposite, almost immediately.  
**Example:**  
- Professor/Teacher: "Please don't say 'moist.'"  
- The class (in unison): "***Moist.***"
